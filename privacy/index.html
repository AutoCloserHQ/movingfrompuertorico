Website policy

Privacy and your request.

MovingFromPuertoRico.com is operated by ModelMoving. This page explains how this website uses information you provide.

Plan my mainland move ↗

Information collected

A quote request includes the origin, destination, date, move size, name, phone and optional email and notes. You may indicate vehicle, packing or storage needs. The site also records the page you visited, campaign parameters and referring site when available. Please avoid including payment card data, identity documents or other sensitive records in notes.

How information is used and shared

ModelMoving uses submitted information to respond to your inquiry and may share relevant contact and move details with selected moving providers so they can discuss pricing, availability and service. Those providers have their own privacy practices. Service providers supporting website hosting, form delivery, analytics and request management may process information to provide those functions.

Calls, texts and emails

By submitting the form with the consent box checked, you agree that ModelMoving and selected moving partners may contact you about this request by phone, text or email. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not required to purchase services. You can ask us to stop contacting you by replying STOP to a text or writing to the address below; a provider may also have its own opt-out process.

Analytics and browser storage

The site uses analytics to understand visits and form interactions. It sends page paths and controlled event names, not your quote fields, to analytics. Campaign and referring-site details may be stored for this browser session and included in a submitted request. An optional checklist saves selected tasks in this browser until you clear them; it does not send the checklist to the quote form.

Retention, access and contact

Inquiry information is retained as needed to handle requests, maintain records and address legal obligations. You may ask about the information you submitted or request correction or deletion where applicable by emailing modelmoving@gmail.com. Please identify the email or phone number used in your request so we can locate it. This policy may be updated as the website changes. Last updated September 26, 2026.

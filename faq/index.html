Common questions

Good questions make better moves.

Start with what you know. A provider can clarify the details for your address, inventory and schedule.

Plan my mainland move ↗

Will the quote include the whole trip?

Ask the provider to describe pickup, packing, ocean transportation and final mainland delivery in its written scope. Do not assume a quote covers each stage unless it says so.

Can I move a car too?

You can indicate a vehicle in your request. Ask about availability for your specific route and whether vehicle handling is a separate service, charge and document set.

How soon should I start?

Begin once your likely addresses and date range are known. Availability and schedules vary, so earlier conversations give you more room to prepare. Do not rely on a fixed transit estimate until your provider confirms its plan.

Who sends the final estimate?

A selected moving provider supplies its own written estimate and booking agreement after reviewing your move. Make sure the company and services are clear before you accept.

Is requesting options free?

Yes. Sending a quote request through this website is free and does not obligate you to book a move.

Ready to make it specific?

Share your Puerto Rico pickup, mainland address and the services you need. A selected provider can discuss availability and prepare its own written proposal.

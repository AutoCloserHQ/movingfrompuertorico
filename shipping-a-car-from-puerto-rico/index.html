Vehicle guide

Bringing your car from Puerto Rico?

A vehicle deserves its own written plan alongside your household move.

Plan my mainland move ↗

Decide how the vehicle fits the move

Tell the provider the year, make, model and whether the car operates normally. State where it is in Puerto Rico and where you want to receive it on the mainland. Ask whether door pickup, terminal drop-off and onward transportation are available for your route.

Prepare before handoff

Ask the actual vehicle shipping provider for its current preparation, identification and document requirements before you go to a drop-off point. Photograph the vehicle’s condition and keep copies of handoff paperwork. Do not assume household boxes can ride in the vehicle.

Coordinate arrival

The car and household goods may be handled under separate arrangements. Ask about each delivery point, the notification process, who can collect the car and what changes if your flight or housing date shifts. Get the agreed scope and charges in writing.

Ready to make it specific?

Share your Puerto Rico pickup, mainland address and the services you need. A selected provider can discuss availability and prepare its own written proposal.

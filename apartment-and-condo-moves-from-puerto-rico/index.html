Access guide

An apartment move has two buildings to plan.

The best time to find a loading restriction is before your moving day.

Plan my mainland move ↗

At the Puerto Rico home

Ask management about move hours, elevator reservations, loading spaces and any required notice. Tell your provider about the floor, stairs, walk from unit to vehicle and any furniture that needs disassembly.

At the mainland home

Check the destination building’s move-in window and loading procedure. Some buildings request advance documentation from the service provider. Give those requirements to the provider before a delivery date is arranged.

If dates do not line up

A lease ending before your receiving home is available creates a gap. Explain it early and ask about storage, access to stored goods and redelivery in writing. Keep an essentials bag with you so that the gap does not become an emergency.

Ready to make it specific?

Share your Puerto Rico pickup, mainland address and the services you need. A selected provider can discuss availability and prepare its own written proposal.

Destination guide

Moving from Puerto Rico to Florida.

Florida is a common next chapter, but the receiving address makes each Puerto Rico move different.

Plan my mainland move ↗

Make the route specific

Start with your pickup municipality in Puerto Rico and your Florida destination. A waterfront condo, a gated community outside Orlando and a single-family home near Tampa have different delivery arrangements. Give the provider the final address, loading rules and the date your home is ready.

Ask whether the written proposal includes pickup in Puerto Rico, ocean transportation, inland Florida delivery and any requested packing or storage.

Build an inventory you can discuss

Room count is a starting point. List large furniture, approximate box count and pieces needing special protection. Mention a vehicle, packing or storage as separate needs. This makes the scope easier to discuss before a provider prepares its written proposal.

If your Florida housing is not finalized, explain the gap before committing to a receiving date. Ask how a change of address or temporary storage would affect the scope.

Understand the handoffs

Ask how collection in Puerto Rico connects with the ocean portion and Florida delivery. Find out who communicates at each stage, when updates are available and what you need to do before pickup. The exact route and schedule depend on the provider and service selected.

Keep passports or IDs, medication, keys, lease paperwork and daily essentials with you during travel. They should not be packed where you cannot reach them.

Ready to make it specific?

Share your Puerto Rico pickup, mainland address and the services you need. A selected provider can discuss availability and prepare its own written proposal.

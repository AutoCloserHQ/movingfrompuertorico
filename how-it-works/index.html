Our process

Your move, clearly connected.

Tell us the route and the work you need. We help put your request in front of a suitable moving provider.

Plan my mainland move ↗

Tell us the move

Enter your Puerto Rico pickup location, mainland destination, timing, move size and best contact details. Include packing, storage, a vehicle or building access in the notes when relevant. There is no charge to submit the request and no obligation to book.

Review and follow-up

ModelMoving reviews the inquiry and may connect you with a selected moving provider based on route, timing and service needs. ModelMoving or a selected provider may follow up using the contact information and consent you supplied. Availability varies by route.

Discuss the written proposal

The provider identifies itself, discusses your inventory and service scope, and provides its own estimate and booking documents. Review the company name, services, price terms and delivery arrangements before making a decision. A website request is not a booking.

Ready to make it specific?

Share your Puerto Rico pickup, mainland address and the services you need. A selected provider can discuss availability and prepare its own written proposal.

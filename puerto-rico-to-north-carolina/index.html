Destination guide

Moving from Puerto Rico to North Carolina.

Start with the North Carolina address you plan to use, even if your move date is still flexible.

Plan my mainland move ↗

Make the route specific

Start with your pickup municipality in Puerto Rico and your North Carolina destination. An uptown apartment, suburban home, mountain road and coastal community present different access questions. Mention elevators, gates, stairs and driveway limitations early.

Confirm whether the proposed service ends at the final North Carolina home and what happens if that home is not ready.

Build an inventory you can discuss

Room count is a starting point. List large furniture, approximate box count and pieces needing special protection. Mention a vehicle, packing or storage as separate needs. This makes the scope easier to discuss before a provider prepares its written proposal.

If a family member will receive delivery, identify them and ask the provider what authorization and scheduling information they will need.

Understand the handoffs

Ask how collection in Puerto Rico connects with the ocean portion and North Carolina delivery. Find out who communicates at each stage, when updates are available and what you need to do before pickup. The exact route and schedule depend on the provider and service selected.

Keep passports or IDs, medication, keys, lease paperwork and daily essentials with you during travel. They should not be packed where you cannot reach them.

Ready to make it specific?

Share your Puerto Rico pickup, mainland address and the services you need. A selected provider can discuss availability and prepare its own written proposal.

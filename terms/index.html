Planning information

Guides and interactive tools are general planning information. They do not provide a binding price, schedule, availability guarantee or individualized transportation advice. Confirm actual requirements and service terms with the provider handling your move.

Requests and provider agreements

A website request is free and does not create a booking. ModelMoving may review the inquiry and connect you with a selected moving provider. A provider identifies itself and issues its own estimate, contract and service terms. Review those documents before you commit.

Using the website

Provide accurate contact and move details and do not submit someone else’s information without authority. Automated misuse and false requests may be blocked. Website text and design may not be republished without permission.

Questions and changes

Contact modelmoving@gmail.com with questions about the website. The terms may change as the service evolves. Last updated September 26, 2026.

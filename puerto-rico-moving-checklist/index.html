Plan my mainland move ↗

Before you request options

List furniture and boxes room by room. Decide what stays, what ships and what travels with you. Identify any vehicle, fragile piece or storage need. Write down both addresses and a realistic date range.

Before pickup

Confirm building or gated access and loading rules at your Puerto Rico home. Ask who handles packing, what documentation you receive and how to update the inventory. Put essentials, medication, IDs, keys and critical documents aside for travel.

Before delivery

Make sure somebody can receive the shipment at the final mainland address. Confirm parking, elevators, permits or entry codes where relevant. Save your service agreement and ask how the provider shares status updates.

Ready to make it specific?

Share your Puerto Rico pickup, mainland address and the services you need. A selected provider can discuss availability and prepare its own written proposal.
